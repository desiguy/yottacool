How to Save on iPhone

Instructions for Contacts & Apple Wallet

1. Save directly to iOS Contacts

  1. Tap the "Save Contact to iPhone (.vcf)" button.
  2. In Safari, tap Download or tap the downloaded file link.
  3. Your iPhone will prompt "Add to Contacts" or open the contact modal.
  4. Tap Save / Done in the top right corner.

2. Apple Wallet Pass (.pkpass)

Native `.pkpass` files require cryptographic signing with an Apple Developer certificate. To convert this digital business card into an official Apple Wallet Pass:

  • Download your .vcf file generated above.
  • Upload it to standard free pass generators (like Pass2U or PassKit).
  • It will instantly generate a barcode or wallet pass for native Apple Wallet!
